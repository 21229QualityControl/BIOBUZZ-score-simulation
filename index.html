Model details and limitations
  • A Hive tip uses configurable progress units. Defaults reproduce 8 Pollen or 5 Nectar: Pollen = 5, Nectar = 8, threshold = 40.
  • At the default TELEOP start, one AUTO tip is already counted, the active Cell is empty, four Nectar are available, and four remain with the Human Player.
  • Before 1:00 remaining, each tip introduces one held Nectar. At 1:00, every remaining held Nectar becomes available.
  • Cycle durations are sampled uniformly from average minus jitter to average plus jitter.
  • Batch reliability follows the requested Y-or-Y−1 model. A failed batch misses exactly one ball.
  • Preset names use “2N” or “3N” for the Nectar target. Actual intake is limited by how many Nectar are currently available.
  • Nectar that scores is unavailable while in the Cell and becomes available after the Hive tips. A missed Nectar returns to the available pool.
  • Hive points equal 20 points per tip plus 2 points for each ball remaining in the active Cell at the end. “Extra points” is the dual-size total minus the Pollen-only total.
  • The simulation does not model defense, travel geometry, Hive settling time, shot distance, or Flower scoring.
